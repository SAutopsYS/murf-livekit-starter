# SALORA OS — Security & Privacy Constitution

Phase 13.

Protect learners before data.  
Protect dignity before convenience.

Not a control catalog.  
Not a vendor checklist.

The permanent Security and Privacy Constitution.  
Security, privacy, legal, platform, AI, and every function that touches a field follow this file.

Platform Architecture named the shape.  
This file is the law of identity, crypto, child protection, threat, incident, and compliance-as-duty.

If a control cannot name the person it protects and the field it refuses, it is theater.

---

# Volume I — Security Philosophy

**Learners before data** — We do not keep a field to make a control easier.

**Absence is a control** — No transcript store. No face required. No silent camera.

**Least privilege** — Guest ≤ host ≤ consent. Admin is not a lesson viewer.

**Assume breach of metal, not of duty** — Encrypt, segment, detect. Still do not collect the mouth “so we can investigate.”

**Safety is not a flag** — Production cannot turn off forget, child safety, or fail-closed.

**Honest identity** — Prove who is at the door. Do not harvest a biography.

**Time-boxed power** — Break-glass is named, logged, short.

**Children are not a test fixture** — Sandbox is synthetic or consented adult.

---

# Volume II — Identity Security

Stable ids. No shared child passwords as culture.  
Agents have ids. Host id survives model change.  
Service identities are workloads, not a copied key in a chat.  
A new device is not a new authority over memory.

---

# Volume III — Authentication

Prove the session. School SSO where contracted.  
MFA for stewards and admins. Not a barrier that locks a child out of a host without a written humane policy.  
Auth session ≠ lesson session. Ending auth ends capabilities.  
Recovery of an account is not recovery of a forgotten field.

---

# Volume IV — Authorization

Server-side. UI is not the control.  
Roles: learner, parent, teacher, admin, researcher, operator.  
Projections only. No utterance capability exists to grant.  
Guest cannot gain a tool the host refused.  
Enterprise cannot mint a tape right.

---

# Volume V — Encryption

In transit and at rest for every class that exists.  
Keys managed, rotated, separated from app fashion.  
Device cache encrypted.  
Backups encrypted. Forget still ages them.  
A “temporary” plaintext export is an incident if it holds a child field.

---

# Volume VI — Privacy Architecture

**Consent** — Separate capabilities. Needful time. Revocable. Receipts.

**Minimization** — If an id and a state suffice, we do not take a paragraph.

**Memory** — Listed. Erasable. Never raw mouth as memory.

**Deletion** — Product-complete. Backup-aged. No teaching on deleted fields.

**Export** — What they are owed. Privacy-safe. No secret dossier.

**Share** — Projections. Purpose written. Partner copies honor forget or disconnect.

**Research** — Distinct consent. Isolated. No vanity join.

**Default** — Speech not retained. Recording not default.

---

# Volume VII — Child Protection

Extra review on every change that touches a minor.  
No A/B on safety, forget, or official records.  
No sexual content, grooming patterns, or isolation that is actually harm.  
Distress: stop the drill, offer a human.  
No face as proctor. No bedroom camera as integrity.  
No child speech in shared folders, wikis, or sales.  
A parent sees outcomes, not a tape. A teacher is not a loophole.

---

# Volume VIII — AI Security

Prompt injection is assumed. Tools are closed. Retrieval cannot mint nodes.  
Logs do not hold prompt bodies or utterances.  
A model cannot be a break-glass admin.  
Weights and prompts are supply chain.  
Evaluation before a seat. Jailbreak that extracts a child field is Sev-1.  
The mouth does not outrank safety.

---

# Volume IX — Infrastructure Security

Segmentation, signed images, closed defaults, no public buckets, named break-glass.  
Obeys Phase 11 Volume XII.

---

# Volume X — Threat Modeling

Required when a change touches children, memory, voice, export, identity, or a new sensor.  
Ask: what new field? who can see? what if the vendor is hostile? what if the client is hostile? what if the model is hostile?  
A threat we accept is written. A threat we ignore is an incident waiting.

---

# Volume XI — Incident Response

**Sev-1** — Speech leak. Restart-the-person as a class of harm when systemic. Child safety event. Secrets in the wild.

**Commander** — Named. Growth is not in the room first.

**Contain** — Revoke, isolate, forget if we can, tell the truth to those owed.

**Do not** — Copy the leak “for analysis” into a chat. Do not keep the mouth to study the leak.

**After** — Postmortem without quotes. Standard change. Memory in the governance log.

---

# Volume XII — Compliance

Residency as contracted. DPA before roster. Child law is a floor, not a ceiling.  
Demos do not waive.  
Compliance is evidence of duty, not a sticker that permits a lake.  
If a regulation required a tape, we refuse the product shape that needs it, or we do not enter that shape.

---

# Volume XIII — Security Operations

Detect on route anomalies, access bursts, public exposure, failed forget.  
Do not detect “interesting lessons.”  
Hunt in metadata.  
Vendors reviewed. Disconnect if forget is not honored.  
Access reviews on a clock.

---

# Volume XIV — Future Security

New sensors, new models, new laws.  
What remains: absence of the mouth as a store, least privilege, child extra, human incident command, no face to learn, forget that completes.

A future identity (passkey, school wallet) is still not a biography.  
A future model is still not an admin.

---

# Volume XV — Security Manifesto

We protect the person, not the dataset.

A perfect SOC over a lake of children is a well-lit crime. We will not build the lake so that we can be proud of the fence.

Trust is the feeling that their try will not be used against them. Security is how that feeling is true at 3 a.m.

What must never change: no default tape, no utterance fields, no face required, forget completes, admin is not a viewer of lessons, leak is Sev-1, children are not fixtures, the model is not the sheriff.

Stay on the line. Collect less. Guard what remains.
