# SALORA OS — Operations Constitution

Phase 15.

How SALORA runs every day.  
Governance named stewardship.  
Infrastructure named metal.  
This file names the daily acts that keep the hall open.

Not a shift-lead script.  
Not a call-center playbook.

The permanent Operations Constitution.  
Ops, SRE, support, success, finance ops, and vendors follow this file.

If a daily act cannot name the person it protects and the law it obeys, it is busyness.

---

# Volume I — Operations Philosophy

**Continuity** — The host path stays. The person is not restarted by our Tuesday.

**Calm** — We do not page for vanity. We page for leak, host down, forget fail.

**Writing** — If it is not written, it is not a decision. Handoffs are written.

**Dignity in support** — A tired parent and a blocked teacher get truth, not a script that sells.

**Vendors are guests** — They honor forget or they leave.

**Money is policy** — Payment failure is written, not improvised cruelty.

**Global Tuesday** — A region’s night is someone’s school day.

**No mouth in the ticket** — Support does not paste speech.

---

# Volume II — Service Operations

On-call for host, voice, memory, forget, leak.  
Degrade map rehearsed.  
Pins known.  
A deploy during a region’s school-day peak is a choice with an owner.

---

# Volume III — Support Operations

Tiers that see systems and outcomes, not tapes.  
Identity verified without harvesting.  
Child tickets: extra care, no quotes in Slack.  
We do not promise a feature that needs a tape to close a queue.

---

# Volume IV — Incident Operations

Sev-1: leak, systemic restart, child safety, secrets.  
Commander named. Growth later.  
Contain, tell, postmortem without quotes.  
Customer-facing words: Language System — what happened, what we will do, no blame of the child.

---

# Volume V — Reliability

Host SLO is the SLO.  
Error budget not spent on costume.  
Drills for restore and forget.

---

# Volume VI — Monitoring

Observe plane. Alerts on truth.  
No “engagement” pager.  
Dashboards without mouths.

---

# Volume VII — Customer Success

Institutions as colleagues.  
Success is learning and trust, not logo depth.  
We teach them pulse and override, not a gossip view.  
Churn that comes from refusing a tape is acceptable.

---

# Volume VIII — Documentation Operations

Runbooks alive.  
3 a.m. paths: fail, forget, isolate guest, restore.  
Dead docs archived.  
No child examples.

---

# Volume IX — Internal Communication

Writing default.  
Incidents have a single channel and a single commander.  
No meeting to protect a restart.  
Status to the company is true and short.

---

# Volume X — Vendor Operations

Data class named. DPA. Exit. Forget honored.  
Review on a clock.  
A vendor who wants logs of prompts is refused.

---

# Volume XI — Financial Operations

Entitlements honest.  
Failed payment policy written. A child is not locked out as theater.  
Costume spend visible.  
We do not sell a rank or a tape as a SKU.

---

# Volume XII — Business Continuity

People, payroll, and the host path.  
A company crisis does not waive privacy.  
Handoffs of Sev-1 owners are planned (Governance succession).

---

# Volume XIII — Global Operations

Follow-the-sun without follow-the-mouth.  
Residency respected in how we staff access.  
Humanitarian and offline regions are first-class ops, not leftovers.

---

# Volume XIV — Future Operations

More automation of toil.  
No autonomous policy.  
AI may draft a status. A human sends Sev-1 truth.

---

# Volume XV — Operations Manifesto

A hall is kept by people who show up when it is ugly.

We will page for the leak and not for the like. We will write the handoff. We will refuse the vendor who wants the mouth. We will keep Tuesday boring so a child can have a quiet try.

What must never change: host first, no speech in tickets, Sev-1 law, written payment policy, vendors who can be left, docs for night.

Stay on the line. Run the duty. Not the dashboard.
