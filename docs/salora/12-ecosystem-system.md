# SALORA OS — Ecosystem System Constitution

Phase 9.

The Constitution defines the purpose.  
The Product Blueprint defines the product.  
The Learning System defines learning.  
The Knowledge System defines knowledge.  
The Content System defines educational resources.

This file defines how every participant, institution, partner, platform, service, and external system collaborates inside the SALORA ecosystem.

Not an organization chart.  
Not a partnership strategy.  
Not an API catalog.

The permanent Ecosystem Architecture and Collaboration Constitution.  
Schools, universities, enterprises, publishers, certification bodies, governments, NGOs, developers, platform architects, researchers, and product follow this file.

Product Architecture named workspaces and roles.  
Platform Architecture named tenancy and services.  
Assessment System named the credential object.

This file is the law of the network around the hall.

If a partner, integration, community, or extension cannot name the learner benefit, the data it may touch, the consent it needs, and the failure path that keeps the host, it does not enter the ecosystem.

# Volume I — Ecosystem Philosophy

An educational ecosystem is a network of duties around a person who is trying.  
It is not a pile of logos. It is not a pipe of children.

**Learning as a network**  
A try is stronger when a teacher, a parent, a peer, and a host share the same thread — outcomes, not tapes. Isolation is a product. A network is a responsibility.

**Human collaboration**  
People teach, assign, override, and care. Machines draft and retrieve. A partnership that replaces the human with a fluent summary has left this constitution.

**Open ecosystems**  
Others may contribute packages, credentials, and tools. Open is not ungoverned. A door that anyone can dump through is not openness. It is a flood.

**Trust before integration**  
A connection that leaks speech, restarts the person, or ranks children is not an integration. It is an incident with a vendor name. We refuse it before we schedule it.

**Institutions as partners**  
Schools, universities, and states are colleagues in a duty, not customers of a surveillance suite. They receive what they are owed. They do not buy a mouth.

**Learners at the center**  
No participant outranks the person the object describes. When a contract and a child’s dignity conflict, the contract is rewritten or refused.

**Distributed education**  
Learning may happen in a school, a home, a workplace, a library, or alone. The OS remains one host, one license, one identity. Distribution is of place, not of soul.

**Interoperability**  
Records, credentials, and content metadata may leave and return. Interoperability is portability of what they own. It is not a back door into the lesson.

**Shared responsibility**  
Learner tries. Teacher judges the class. Parent supports conditions. Institution holds policy. Partner holds their package. SALORA holds the thread and the refuse. None owns the mouth.

**Lifelong educational communities**  
A person may change school, city, and decade. Communities may persist if they still serve a try and still allow exit. A community that cannot be left is a trap.

# Volume II — Ecosystem Architecture

Every participant is one of these kinds.  
A brand is not a kind.

**Learner** — The person practicing. Center. Owns licensed memory and learning records.

**Parent** — Care relationship. Sees time, topic, next step. Never a tape.

**Teacher** — Professional relationship. Assigns, overrides, explains, signs official samples.

**School** — Tenancy of classrooms, teachers, policy, roster. Contract and retention.

**University** — Higher-ed tenancy. Credit and research under their warrant. Not a childhood archive.

**Tutor** — The Host in the room. Not a partner logo. Not replaceable by a guest.

**Mentor** — Human or Mentor seat, by request. Not a parent replacement. Not a life owner.

**Institution** — A tenancy with policy: school, university, training body, library system. Named type required.

**Enterprise** — Company or district above or beside schools. Contracts, health, aggregates. No speech toy.

**Government** — Regulator or program sponsor. May require residency, standards, and audit. May not require a tape.

**Certification body** — Issues a claim to the world. Human path. Validity evidence. Decay or maintenance stated.

**Publisher** — Contributes content and knowledge packages. Passes Content and Knowledge governance.

**Research organization** — Studies under protocol. Distinct consent. No vanity join to product analytics.

**Developer** — Builds extensions that obey this file and the Constitution. Sandbox first.

**Community** — A voluntary group around a try (Volume VII). Exit is first-class.

## Relationships

| Edge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `cares-for` | Parent → learner |
| `teaches` | Teacher → learner or class |
| `enrolls` | Learner → school, course, program |
| `employs` | Institution → teacher |
| `sponsors` | Government or enterprise → program |
| `issues` | Certification body → credential |
| `publishes` | Publisher → package |
| `extends` | Developer → allowed capability |
| `projects-to` | Outcome projection, never utterance |
| `consents` | Learner or guardian → a share or a study |
| `overrides` | Teacher → guest or path |
| `audits` | Authority → access and policy, not mouths |

A relationship that needs a tape is not a relationship we implement.  
A participant without a typed edge is a logo in a slide.

# Volume III — Institutional Architecture

An institution is a tenancy with a duty.  
It has ownership, a boundary, and a refuse.

**Schools** — Roster, classroom, assignment, pulse, override. Own policy for members as law allows. Do not own a child’s mouth. Export on leave.

**Universities** — Credit, programs, research. Separate adult records from any prior child record. Do not mine a freshman for a childhood.

**Coaching centers** — Allowed as institutions if they take the same privacy and override duties. A center that wants a leaderboard of children is refused.

**Training organizations** — Adult and professional paths. Same evidence rules. No child roster as a side database.

**Enterprises** — Skills of workers, or districts of schools. Aggregates and health. A manager does not receive a worker’s lesson speech.

**Government programs** — National or state paths, residency, audit of access. A program that requires face-proctoring of children in bedrooms is refused.

**NGOs** — Humanitarian and access programs. Offline and low-bandwidth are first-class. Dignity does not shrink because the budget did.

**Libraries** — Knowledge and content access. Not a surveillance desk. A library card is not a personality profile.

**Learning communities** — Voluntary, exitible, hosted as Community (Volume VII), not as a silent tenancy.

**Research institutes** — Protocol, IRB-equivalent, distinct consent. Results may improve the OS. Participants are not content.

## Ownership, boundaries, responsibilities

| | Owns | Must not |
|---|---|---|
| Institution | Roster, policy, assignments, contract data | Utterances, secret observers, shadow graphs after leave beyond contract |
| SALORA | Host, license engine, fail-closed, platform | The person’s life plan, the institution’s professional judgment |
| Publisher | Their package versions | A second host, uncited live claims |
| Learner | Memory, records, export, delete | Being made responsible for our leak |

When an institution leaves, they take what they are owed. They do not keep a hidden profile we built from speech.

# Volume IV — Collaboration Architecture

Collaboration supports learning, not bureaucracy.  
A meeting that does not change a next try is not a collaboration we productize.

**Teacher–learner** — Assign, feedback, override, conference. Items and outcomes. No tape.

**Parent–learner** — Conditions and encouragement. One next step at a table. Not a second examiner.

**Mentor–learner** — By request. Longer horizon. Leaves. Does not become host.

**AI–teacher** — Drafts, pulse, suggested path. Teacher sends and signs. AI does not message a child as a warden.

**Teacher–teacher** — Shared class, shared rubric, handoff of a cohort. Still no gossip view.

**School–parent** — Time, topic, next step, rare safety. Language another parent could hear at a door.

**Institution–institution** — Credit transfer, roster trust, package share. Typed edges. No scrape of learners.

**Enterprise–university** — Adult competency and credit. Separate from any child graph.

**Community learning** — Circles and cohorts with presence. Host chairs if AI is in the room.

**Peer learning** — Teach-others, shared tasks. Visible presence. A dropped peer fails toward the host. We do not grade a child for a ghost.

Collaboration that adds a silent observer is refused.  
Collaboration that adds nineteen approval steps before a hint is bureaucracy. Cut it.

# Volume V — Integration Architecture

Integrations extend SALORA. They do not redefine it.  
The host, the license, and fail-closed are not negotiable in a statement of work.

**Student information systems** — Roster, enroll, leave. Least fields. No lesson pipe.

**Learning management systems** — Assignment and outcome pass-back. We are not a widget that forgets the person. They are not a second tutor.

**Identity providers** — Prove who is at the door. School SSO where contracted. Identity is not a biography harvest.

**Calendar systems** — Due revisions and sessions they set. Not streak threats.

**Communication platforms** — Rare, factual, quiet. Not a second tutor. Not marketing.

**Video platforms** — Optional content twins. Captions. Not a required spectacle. Not a proctor camera.

**Cloud storage** — Licensed resources they meant to keep. Not an ambient scrape of the home.

**Assessment providers** — May supply items if they pass Assessment and Knowledge law. They do not own the child’s official record without the institution’s human path.

**Digital libraries** — Retrieval of cited objects. License honored. Not a silent child-web.

**Payment systems** — Entitlements. Written policy on failed payment. No humiliation of a child at the door of a host that already knew them.

Rules:

- An integration names the data class (Volume XI) and the workspace it may touch.
- An integration that needs a transcript field is refused.
- An integration that restarts the person on return is refused.
- A vendor outage fails toward the host, not toward a blank stranger.
- Dual-write of the same attempt is a defect.

# Volume VI — Credential Ecosystem

Assessment System defined the credential object.  
This volume is the network of issuers and verifiers.

**Schools** — May issue local claims with a human path. Portable as the person exports.

**Universities** — Academic credit under their warrant. We do not invent credit.

**Employers** — May verify a passport the person presented. They do not browse our graph.

**Certification providers** — Bind to competencies, validity evidence, maintenance. No attendance certificates.

**Government credentials** — When a state issues, we carry the claim as they define it, still without a tape.

**Professional licenses** — Adult. Separate store from childhood.

**Digital credentials** — Verifiable issuance, not a replay of the attempt. Revocable. Forget-compatible.

**Academic credit** — Institution signs. Graph version named.

**Competency records** — The honest object. Decay visible.

**Lifelong learning records** — The person holds the bundle. Schools do not keep a shadow after leave beyond contract.

Credentials remain verifiable and portable.  
Verifiable does not mean public.  
Portable does not mean a vendor can hoard the only copy.

# Volume VII — Community Architecture

Communities exist to support learning with dignity.  
They are voluntary. Exit is obvious. Presence is visible.

**Study groups** — Shared try. Host available. No silent adults.

**Learning circles** — Recurring, small, named purpose.

**Clubs** — Interest around a domain. Still a try, not a brand fandom.

**Cohorts** — Time-boxed together (a course). End is honest. Alumni is a new, optional community.

**Alumni** — Optional. No harvesting of a child’s yearbook into a product.

**Research communities** — Protocol. Adults or consented studies. Not a child forum.

**Teacher communities** — Practice, rubrics, packages. No student named without a need and a right.

**Parent communities** — Support, not a comparison wall. No ranks of children.

**Developer communities** — Extensions, sandboxes, reviews. No production data as a sample.

**Global communities** — Locale-respecting. English is not the default soul.

A community that ranks, shames, or records by default is dissolved.  
A community that cannot be left has failed Volume I.

# Volume VIII — Marketplace Architecture

The marketplace is a governed catalog of contributions.  
It is not a mall of personalities.  
It is not a store of souls.

Allowed contribution kinds:

**Courses / lessons** — Content packages that `present` live knowledge objects.

**AI extensions** — Named guests or tools with a subgraph, evaluation seat, way home. No costume without curriculum.

**Subject experts** — Guest packages, not influencers.

**Assessments** — Item sets with validity evidence and human-path flags.

**Templates** — Lesson spines, rubrics, classroom structures. Empty of children.

**Classroom resources** — Worksheets and twins of the same ids.

**Accessibility resources** — Twins, captions, large-type packages.

**Research tools** — In the Research workspace. Not on a child’s Home.

**Educational plugins** — Developer extensions (Volume X).

Every contribution requires validation before distribution: Knowledge, Content, Assessment, AI, and this file’s governance.  
A partner who fails child safety, citation, or forget is removed, not “given time to grow.”

We do not sell:

- Host replacements
- Default recording
- Leaderboards of children
- Face-as-proctor products
- Uncited live science
- Personality packs

If a listing cannot name the learner’s next try, it is not a listing.

# Volume IX — Governance Architecture

Governance protects learners before partners.  
A large logo does not shorten a review.

**Partner approval** — Named org, duty, data classes, regions, owners. Harm analysis. Refuse is a success.

**Accreditation** — We do not pretend to be a state. We may require partners who issue credentials to show their warrant.

**Compliance** — Residency, DPA, child law, labor of teachers. Demos do not waive it.

**Quality assurance** — Live packages monitored for item-fault, leaks, and restart defects. Kill switches exist.

**Editorial governance** — Content and Knowledge boards outrank a partner’s marketing.

**AI governance** — Seats, pins, evaluation. A partner model occupies a seat or it does not speak.

**Data sharing** — Projections only. Written purpose. Revocable. No “for improvement” that means a tape.

**Privacy** — Same as Platform and Constitution. A partner is not a loophole.

**Licensing** — Packages and APIs licensed. We do not launder a stolen work through a partner.

**Audit history** — Who joined, what they can touch, what they exported, what they lost. Access, not speech.

A dispute between revenue and a child’s dignity is not a dispute. Dignity wins. Then we write the decision.

# Volume X — Developer Ecosystem

Extensions must obey the Constitution.  
A clever API that breaks the host is not a platform win.

**APIs** — Smallest payload. Versioned. No utterance fields. Errors structured.

**SDKs** — Same law. Cannot request a transcript capability that does not exist.

**Plugins / extensions** — Registered, permissioned, sandboxed. Guest ≤ host ≤ consent.

**Automation** — Roster and assignment sync. Not a bot in the child’s mouth.

**Webhooks** — Event names, not content. Retry-safe. No speech payloads.

**AI tools** — Closed, validated, logged without bodies. Tool Runtime law.

**Third-party services** — Volume V. Fail toward host.

**Testing sandboxes** — Synthetic or consented adult data. No real child roster.

**Versioning** — Pins. Breaking changes cannot reset a person or drop forget.

A developer who needs to “just log the prompt” is asking for an incident.  
The Developer Layer in the Product Blueprint is this volume in motion.

# Volume XI — Data Exchange

Data exchange follows learner ownership.  
The person can see what left, or it should not have left.

**Learning records** — States, classes, dates. Exportable. Privacy-safe.

**Progress** — Journey and mastery level, including decay.

**Competencies** — Claims and evidence pointers (item ids), not answers as speech.

**Credentials** — Issued objects. Verifiable. Portable.

**Attendance** — If an institution requires it: presence of a session as a visit, not a recording.

**Assessments** — Outcomes and, if official, item ids. No oral tape.

**Content metadata** — Titles, bindings, versions, licenses. Not the child’s notes unless they chose to export them.

**Knowledge metadata** — Object ids, edges, versions. The graph is not a child.

**Consent** — Receipts travel with the share. A share without a receipt is a leak.

**Audit trails** — Who pulled what class of data. Not the data itself when the data is a mouth.

Rules:

- Default is do not share.
- School share is outcomes, not utterances.
- Research share is protocol, not product telemetry joined for fun.
- A partner who stores a copy must honor forget or they are disconnected.
- Dual systems must not mint two tutors or two scores for one try.

# Volume XII — Global Education Architecture

Global expansion never compromises educational principles.  
A country is not a reason to add a tape.

**National curricula** — Bind as edges and paths. Do not fork the host.

**Regional standards** — `equivalent-to`. Exam form named when it differs.

**Educational frameworks** — Bloom, boards, competency frames map into our objects. They do not replace dignity.

**Language support** — First-class locales. Native script. Hindi → Devanagari. A language we cannot govern waits.

**Accessibility** — Same law in every region. A poorer region is not a reason to drop twins.

**Cultural context** — Local examples. No single-city default as “global.”

**Regulatory compliance** — Residency and audit of access. Architecture allows region. Sales do not waive it.

**Offline regions** — Honest offline, cached last lesson, licensed snapshot, sync without duplicate attempts. First-class, not a lesser product.

**Emerging economies** — Low-end devices and low bandwidth are design targets (Design System). Beauty that costs the try is vanity.

**Humanitarian education** — NGO and emergency programs. Short, stoppable, no harvest of the vulnerable. Identity may be thinner. Dignity is not.

We do not run a different Constitution per market.  
We may run a different path, locale, and residency.

# Volume XIII — Ecosystem Analytics

Analytics improve the ecosystem. They do not create a league table of schools or children.

**Institutional growth** — Tenancies that can teach and forget. Not logo count.

**Learning outcomes** — Transfer, retention, mastery that admits decay, across a tenancy. Aggregates.

**Collaboration quality** — Override used, conferences held, parent lines sent — not message volume.

**Integration reliability** — Fail-toward-host rate, duplicate-attempt rate, field-minimization.

**Community health** — Exit rate without shame, presence visible, rank-and-shame incidents at zero.

**Credential adoption** — Valid claims issued and maintained, not badges minted.

**Research impact** — Studies that changed a path or an item, under protocol.

**Accessibility adoption** — Twin coverage in partner packages.

**Content exchange** — Packages that moved transfer, not downloads.

**Educational impact** — Did the network make the next try cheaper, clearer, more honest? If not, the partnership is decoration.

No public rank of schools.  
No public rank of teachers.  
No child as a case study in a sales deck.

# Volume XIV — Future Ecosystem

Institutions will change shape. The center will not.

**Global learning networks** — Shared packages and `equivalent-to` maps. Still governed. Still no scrape.

**AI-native institutions** — May use more guests. Still one host. Still teacher override. An institution with no humans accountable for official claims is not a partner.

**Distributed universities** — Credit across places. The person holds the record. The university signs. We do not become the university.

**Personal learning networks** — People they chose. Visible. Exitible. Not a shadow graph we built from a tape.

**Autonomous learning communities** — Self-run circles. Still our safety, still our refuse, still no default record of mouths.

**Global competency exchanges** — Portable claims. Decay honest. No public childhood.

**Open educational graphs** — Shared knowledge objects with licenses. Open is not “models may ingest children.”

**Lifelong identity** — One person, many institutions, same host duty. Identity is not a dossier.

**Cross-planet education** — If the hall is farther away, latency and offline grow. The host, the license, and dignity do not thin with the air.

**Future educational collaboration** — New partner kinds inherit Volume II types or they wait for an amendment.

The future may add a kind of institution we cannot name.  
It may not add a partner who buys a tape, a network that ranks children, or a marketplace of hosts.

# Volume XV — Ecosystem Manifesto

Education is larger than any institution.

A school is a season. A university is a warrant. A state is a law. A company is a tool. The person who is trying is the only constant. When we build a network that forgets that, we have built a market of children and called it scale. This hall will not scale that way.

Collaboration creates stronger learning than isolation because a try needs a next item, a human who can override, and sometimes a parent who can make the table quiet. Isolation is a product that fits in a pocket. Collaboration is a duty that fits in a life. We will do the duty. We will not replace it with a feed of partners.

Openness requires responsibility. An open door without a refuse is how a flood enters. We will be open to packages, credentials, and tools that can name their evidence and their failure path. We will be closed to charm, to rank, to cameras in bedrooms, and to “just this once” in a statement of work.

Trust is the foundation of every educational partnership. Trust is not a slide. It is the absence of a tape, the presence of forget, the host that does not become a stranger, and a contract that can be left. A partner who needs our learners more than they need our refuse is not a partner.

Learners remain at the center of every ecosystem. Every edge in this file points, in the end, at a person who must still think. If a new connection does not make their next attempt cheaper, clearer, or more honest, it is not a connection. It is a logo.

What must never change, even when institutions, vendors, and borders change:

The learner is the center.  
Institutions see outcomes, not mouths.  
Integrations extend; they do not redefine the host.  
Credentials are demonstrated classes, portable and decaying.  
Communities are voluntary and exitible.  
The marketplace is a governed catalog, not a store of souls.  
Developers obey the Constitution or they do not ship.  
Data that leaves is data they could see leaving.  
A region is not a reason to weaken dignity.  
Governance protects the child before the partner.

The operating system is a hall with many doors.

If the doors are honest, a city of schools can share the table.  
If the doors are pipes, we have only built a bigger leak.

Stay on the line.  
Name the duty.  
Refuse the tape.  
Keep the person in the center.
