# SALORA OS — Experience Blueprint

How SALORA should feel.

Not a UI document.  
Not a component library.  
Not implementation.

The emotional, cognitive, behavioral, and experiential law of every interaction.

Product, Design, Research, Motion, Writing, Voice, and AI use this file.  
If a feeling cannot be named here, it is not an intended feeling.

---

# Volume I — Experience Philosophy

A learning experience is a relationship under load. The person is trying. The OS is the room.

**Calm**  
One primary. No competing motion. No extra voice. Calm is the feeling that the next act is obvious and the rest can wait. Anxiety is a design defect when we caused it.

**Clear**  
The person can say what this surface is for in one sentence. If they cannot, the experience is unfinished.

**Intentional**  
Every element earns its place by serving the attempt, the memory license, or the way back. Intention is felt as the absence of leftover objects.

**Encouraging**  
After a miss, the person still has a next try that is possible. Encouragement is a smaller item or a true hint, not a slogan.

**Predictable**  
The same act does the same thing tomorrow. Return does not change the host’s personality. Predictable is how trust enters the body.

**Respectful**  
No cute shame. No baby talk to a mind that is working. No performance of intimacy. Respect is felt as adult-to-adult even when the learner is a child: simple words, full dignity.

**Private**  
The body should feel unwatched. Instruments that exist for teachers and parents are labeled. Silence from us about their speech is part of the feeling of the room.

**Continuous**  
Leaving and returning feels like the same day of a life, not a new install. Continuity is a sensation in the first three seconds.

**Trustworthy**  
When something fails, the OS tells the truth and keeps the last good state. Trust is felt most in error, not in delight.

These nine are the intended climate. Humor, spectacle, and “delight” are allowed only when they do not steal a seat from the nine.

# Volume II — Emotional Journey

What the person may feel. What the OS does. The OS does not fake the feeling. It changes the conditions.

**First launch**  
Likely: caution, hope, slight fear of being tested.  
Do: one language, one needful ask, one short attempt. Do not interview their life.

**First lesson**  
Likely: self-consciousness (especially voice).  
Do: the host speaks as if this is normal work. No audience implied. Stop is obvious.

**First mistake**  
Likely: heat, shrinking, urge to leave.  
Do: name the miss, offer a smaller try or one hint. Do not praise the pain. Do not pile explanation.

**First success**  
Likely: relief more than pride.  
Do: confirm the fact. One next step or enough. Do not throw a festival that teaches them we were starving for their win.

**Returning after failure**  
Likely: avoidance, shame.  
Do: same host, same thread, no recap of their character. The item is smaller or spaced, not a sermon.

**Exam preparation**  
Likely: pressure, bargaining, fatigue.  
Do: shorten sessions, keep dignity, show the class of skill not their worth. Rest is a valid end.

**Long-term progress**  
Likely: quiet competence or quiet doubt.  
Do: show states and due work without a personality story. Mastery can fade; say so without drama.

**Completion**  
Likely: closure, suspicion (“is this real”).  
Do: name what was sampled. Do not mint a false forever.

**Frustration**  
Likely: anger at self or at us.  
Do: reduce load, offer stop, offer human if distress. Do not match their heat. Do not joke.

**Confidence**  
Likely: willingness to try a harder item.  
Do: fade hints (expertise reversal). Do not keep them in easy theater to protect a metric.

The OS never requires a mood. It requires a next honest act.

# Volume III — Cognitive Experience

**Attention**  
One object of work. Notifications yield. Guests are announced, then they work, then they leave.

**Focus**  
The try is protected. Chrome recedes during production. Instruments wait.

**Working memory**  
One number, one clause, one step in speech. Do not stack conditions. Write what must be compared.

**Mental models**  
Host, guest, memory list, session, way back. If a new model is required, teach it once with an act, not a paragraph.

**Decision fatigue**  
The OS chooses the next item when they asked it to plan. When they must choose, the set is small and named.

**Flow**  
Possible in practice when items sit at the edge and feedback is the next sentence. We do not manufacture flow with motion.

**Reflection**  
After the set, one beat. During the try, no journal.

**Recovery**  
Cognitive recovery is the same thread plus a smaller demand. Not a new map.

**Confidence**  
Built by accurate feedback and faded support. Destroyed by false praise and surprise difficulty.

**Curiosity**  
Allowed. Not extracted. A question they ask is honored; a question we force for “engagement” is load.

Unnecessary load is anything that is not the subject, the next act, or safety. Remove it.

# Volume IV — Experience Across Time

**First minute**  
They know who is speaking, what to do, how to stop. Continuity is promised by the absence of a tour.

**First day**  
They completed one real attempt. They can leave. If they licensed memory, they can see a field. They were not harvested.

**First week**  
A due item can appear. Return has no new origin story. A miss was recovered without shame.

**First month**  
A goal can exist. A parent or teacher, if invited, saw an outcome they can repeat. The host still sounds like the same room.

**First year**  
Forgotten and mastered have both been true. Forget still works. A model may have changed; the relationship did not restart.

**Five years**  
They can open the OS and be known only as they still allow. Old subjects rest. New subjects do not require a new personality. The body still expects calm.

Continuity across time is the product. Novelty is a guest.

# Volume V — Multimodal Experience

One identity. One host. One license. Mode is a doorway, not a new self.

**Voice**  
Default lesson channel. Pace of a careful teacher. Silence is allowed. Barge-in is theirs for stop, help, safety.

**Touch**  
Large enough commitments. Thumb can Stop. No essential hover.

**Keyboard**  
Full path for the role. Order is meaning.

**Mouse**  
Precision for instruments. Not required to learn.

**Stylus**  
Optional for writing and diagrams. The skill remains available without it unless the skill is handwriting itself.

**Wearables**  
Glance and control. Not a tiny classroom.

**Vision devices**  
Pointing may help. Face and gaze are never the price of entry.

**Future interfaces**  
May add a sense. May not add a required confession.

If they switch mode mid-session, they are not reintroduced. The attempt remains the attempt.

# Volume VI — Feedback Philosophy

Feedback is information for the next try. It is not a show.

**Positive feedback**  
Name what was correct. Short. Then the next act or enough.

**Corrections**  
Name the miss. Do not name the person. Offer the next smaller act.

**Mistakes**  
Expected. Logged as state, not as character.

**Hints**  
One at a time. Fade with success. A hint that is the answer is not a hint; call it the solution and only on request.

**Celebrations**  
Rare. Proportional. Never a sound that startles. Never a public feed.

**Warnings**  
True risk or true loss (forget, end, pay, grant). Not a streak threat.

**Waiting**  
The shape of what is coming, or a short spoken hold. Not a personality skit.

**Loading**  
Honest. If we cannot, we say we cannot.

**Completion**  
What ended. What is next or that nothing is required.

**Progress**  
State and due. Not a cartoon of a life.

If feedback can be removed and the next try is still possible, consider removing it.

# Volume VII — Trust Experience

How trust should feel. Not the legal text.

**Permissions**  
Asked at need. The reason is true. Refusal leaves the room intact. The body should not feel tricked.

**Privacy**  
Unwatched. No quote of their mouth on a wall. Relief when they open Memory and see only what they gave.

**Memory**  
Owned. Listed. Forget is a completed act they can witness.

**AI transparency**  
They know a guest’s name when it enters. They know when we are unsure. They never meet a hidden second mind on a path we called simple.

**Identity**  
The same person across rooms. A role switch does not turn them into a customer of themselves.

**Data**  
Export is something they can hold. It does not contain a secret dossier.

**Offline**  
Told the truth. Not dangled. Not shamed.

**Errors**  
The world stays. The words are plain. Blame is absent. A way forward exists or we say it does not.

Trust is the feeling that we will not use their try against them.

# Volume VIII — Accessibility Experience

Beyond compliance: how access should feel.

**Visual**  
Hierarchy survives without color. Type can grow. Motion can stop and meaning remains. Dark is not a dim puzzle.

**Auditory**  
Voice is intelligible and paced. Captions exist when speech is essential. The OS is not only sound and not only sight.

**Motor**  
Commitments are reachable. Time to tap is humane. Gesture is never the only path.

**Cognitive**  
One step. Plain words. No traps. Predictable return. Working memory respected.

**Language**  
Native script. Mixed language allowed. Switching does not restart the person.

**Age**  
A child is not mocked with baby theater. An elder is not mocked with youth theater. Both get the same dignity and a load they can carry.

**Digital literacy**  
First launch does not assume our map. The primary is visible. Search exists for those who forget maps.

**Bandwidth**  
Low bandwidth is a first-class path: shorter voice, honest wait, no fake richness.

**Device constraints**  
A small phone can finish Core. A shared TV does not expose a private memory edit.

**Offline**  
A constrained network is not a moral failure. The experience is smaller and true.

Accessibility should feel like the room was built for this body, not like a special door at the back.

# Volume IX — Experience Review Framework

Before an experience ships, the room answers. Opinion is not a vote. If an answer is “we will add that later” on Trust, Learning, Recovery, Privacy, or Voice fail-closed, the review fails.

## Emotion

1. What emotion is intended, in one word?
2. What emotion is likely if we caused load?
3. Does first mistake leave a next possible try?
4. Does first success avoid a festival?
5. Does return after failure avoid character talk?
6. Is frustration offered Stop and, if needed, a human?
7. Is exam pressure met with shorter, not louder?
8. Is completion honest about what was sampled?
9. Is humor absent where shame could hide?
10. Would a tired person feel hurried by us?

## Trust

11. Is the reason for each permission true?
12. Can they refuse without losing the host?
13. Is any utterance shown as a quote?
14. Is Memory a list they can read?
15. Does Forget complete in the product they use?
16. Is a guest named on enter and leave?
17. Is uncertainty spoken as uncertainty?
18. Does error keep last good state?
19. Is offline spoken as offline?
20. Could this be used against the learner in a report?

## Learning

22. Is there one difficulty at a time?
23. Is the next item from the last miss or a written plan?
24. Is a solved step not a speech?

26. Is assessment scoring the attempt, not the person?
27. Is there a human path for a permanent record?
28. Are hints faded with success?

101. Is any transcript a dashboard field? If yes, fail.
102. Are phones, OTPs, secrets absent?

# Volume X — Experience Manifesto

Someone will use this hall for decades.

They will change schools, cities, voices in the pipe, and the names of models. They will forget subjects and return. They will be a child and then not a child. They will be tired more often than they will be delighted.

What they should meet, every time:

A room that does not compete with itself.  
A tutor that does not need to be re-introduced.  
A memory they can read and erase.  
A miss that becomes a smaller try.  
A failure of our systems that does not become a failure of their dignity.  
A way to stop.  
A way back.

What must not change when the glass changes, when the model changes, when the school changes:

We do not restart the person.  
We do not watch them for sport.  
We do not shout when they are learning.  
We do not sell their mouth.  
We do not make intelligence a performance.  
We do not confuse a score with a soul.

The experience of SALORA is the feeling of being allowed to continue.

That is the whole climate.  
Everything else is weather.
