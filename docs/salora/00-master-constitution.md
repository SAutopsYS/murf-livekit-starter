# SALORA OS — Master Constitution

Phase 20.

The single highest written authority for SALORA OS.

Every phase (1–19), every product, service, team, model, platform, and partner derives its authority from this file and must comply with it.

The earlier Constitution spoken in leadership is absorbed here as law.  
If a domain constitution and this file disagree, this file wins.  
If a slide and this file disagree, this file wins.  
If a contract and this file disagree on a child’s dignity, the contract is rewritten or refused.

Not a brand book.  
Not a strategy memo.

The permanent operating constitution of the hall.

---

# Volume I — Constitutional Philosophy

A constitution exists so a stranger, in a decade we will not see, can refuse a tape without calling a founder.

It is short enough to hold and complete enough to bind.  
It outlives offices.  
It may be amended in the open.  
It may not be edited in the dark.

SALORA is a learning operating system. The unit of value is a life of practice that does not require re-introduction. Intelligence is a teacher in a hall, not a genie in a box. Institutions are partners in a duty, not buyers of a mouth.

---

# Volume II — Foundational Principles

These laws are permanent unless amended by Volume IV.

**I. The attempt is sovereign.**  
The next try is the point of the product. Features that do not make the attempt cheaper, clearer, or more honest are not features.

**II. The person is not reset.**  
Resume, reconnect, device change, and return after months do not mint a stranger. A new hello after return is a defect.

**III. The tutor is the host.**  
Guests are named, tasked, and sent home. No guest-of-guest. No second kernel.

**IV. Fail toward the tutor.**  
Guest, model, tool, vendor, and network fail into the host, a typed path, or an honest stop — never into a blank stranger or a hang.

**V. Memory is licensed.**  
Listed. Erasable. Never a fact we were not given. Forget completes in the product.

**VI. No default tape.**  
Speech is not a dashboard field, not a warehouse, not a training lake by default. Institutions see outcomes, not mouths.

**VII. Safety sits in front of every mouth.**  
Child harm, distress, medical play, and crime how-tos stop the drill and offer a human. Safety is not a production flag.

**VIII. They produce before we tell, when the goal is skill.**  
A stolen proof is not teaching. Assessment samples a class, not a soul.

**IX. One voice path.**  
Reconnect is not handoff. A second pipeline is an incident.

**X. Dignity outranks the quarter.**  
No rank of children. No face required to learn. No payment theater that humiliates a child at a host that already knew them.

**XI. Evidence outranks confidence.**  
Knowledge is typed, cited, versioned. Retrieval finds; it does not invent. AI drafts; humans publish curriculum and official claims.

**XII. Trust and Learning cannot be zero.**  
A ship that scores zero on either does not ship.

# Volume IV — Amendment Process

1. Written proposal: which law, why, who is harmed, who is helped.  
2. Harm analysis by Trust & Safety, Privacy, and Learning Science when the change touches a child, a field, or a path.  
3. Dissent recorded.  
4. Approval by the steward body of the day (board or successor) plus the functions that hold the refuse being changed.  
5. Versioned. Dated. In the Organizational Memory.  
6. Domain constitutions updated or marked void where they conflict.

A quiet edit is void.  
A model may not amend this file.  
A buyer may not amend Volume II without the same path. If they need a tape, they should have been refused.

---

# Volume V — Conflict Resolution

Order of authority:

1. This Master Constitution (Volume II especially)  
2. Security, Privacy, Child, and Safety clauses in domain files  
3. Learning, Knowledge, Assessment, Content  
4. AI, Platform, Engineering, Data  
5. Product, Experience, Design, Language  
6. Ecosystem, Operations, Research, Quality, Legal, Impact  
7. Operating Standards and playbooks  
8. Tickets, slides, and charm

When two domains conflict, the Constitution hierarchy of interests decides:  
**Human → Learning → Trust → Experience → Technology → Business → Growth → Revenue.**

Rank does not invert this order.  
A Sev-1 commander may act first and write after, but may not keep a mouth “for the file.”

# Volume VI — Constitutional Interpretation

Interpret for continuity, dignity, and the next try.  
Do not interpret for a loophole.

“Transcript” includes any recoverable sequence of what was said.  
“Restart” includes a new origin story, a new host personality as a stranger, and a forked live session.  
“Child” includes anyone we treat as a minor under the stricter of local law and our extra review.  
“Official” means a record that follows the person or faces the world.

If a word is missing, Volume VIII. If still missing, write an amendment or do not ship.

# Volume VIII — Permanent Definitions

**SALORA OS** — The learning operating system: one identity, one host, one license, many rooms.

**Learner** — The person practicing. Center of the ecosystem.

**Steward** — A human who holds a refuse or a Sev-1. Replaceable. The duty is not.

**Live** — Retrievable for teaching or official use. Drafts are not live.

**Enough** — A valid end to a session. Also: the written threshold for a prerequisite or a credential.

**Sev-1** — Speech leak; systemic restart-the-person; child safety event; secrets in the wild.

# Volume IX — Decision Framework

Before a ship, a deal, a seat, or a share, answer:

1. Which Volume II law applies?  
2. Who benefits? Who could be harmed?  
3. What is the fail-toward-host path?  
4. What field is new? Is it licensed?  
5. Can a child and an elder understand the act?  
6. If removed, is the attempt worse?  
7. Who is the owner at 3 a.m.?  
8. What evidence? What review?  
9. Does Trust or Learning score zero? If yes, stop.  
10. Is this written in the log?

If an answer is “we’ll fix trust later,” the decision has failed.

# Volume X — Governance Across Constitutions

The board (or steward body) guards this file.  
Functions guard their refuses (Governance Volume II).  
No feature bypasses constitutional review (Governance Volume IV).  
Standards amend on purpose (Governance Volume VII).  
Partners enter under Ecosystem law, which cannot outrank Volume II.

A function vacancy on Privacy, Trust & Safety, Host/voice, or Forget is an incident.

# Volume XI — Versioning

This file versions.  
Domain files version.  
Pins in production (models, prompts, guests, content) version.  
A version that cannot be named is drift.

Cite as: `Master Constitution vN, law k`.  
Domain files cite the law they obey.

# Volume XII — Constitutional Review

Scheduled review: did we skip a path? Did a deal invert the hierarchy? Did a model sit without a seat? Did a column appear that looks like a mouth?

Written. Dissent recorded.  
Amend or reaffirm.  
A review that requires listening to children to “be thorough” is the wrong review.

---

# Volume XIII — Institutional Memory

The refuse log, the incident class history, the ADRs, and this file’s versions are the memory of the institution.

New stewards read them before they hold a ship button.  
Leadership change is not a factory reset.  
We remember decisions. We do not remember mouths.

# Volume XIV — Future Stewardship

Legal form may change (company, cooperative, trust).  
The duty may not.

AI may draft. It may not sit as steward of this file.  
A generation of learners may become stewards. They should find the same twelve laws, not a store.

If the hall is rebuilt on other metal, other models, other worlds — rebuild the laws first. Then the rooms.

# Volume XV — Final Manifesto

This is the last word only in rank, not in time.

We wrote so many books so that a tired person would not have to guess. Then we wrote this one so the books could not wander.

The hall is a table, a voice, a next item, a way to stop, and a memory they can erase. Everything else is a guest.

Markets will shout. Models will glow. Institutions will ask for a pipe. Founders will leave. Buyers will smile.

The answer remains:

Do not restart the person.  
Do not keep the tape.  
Do not steal the try.  
Do not rank the child.  
Do not sell the host.  
Fail toward the tutor.  
Write the decision.  
Refuse in the open.

If these lines hold, SALORA is still an operating system for learning.  
If they become a poster, it is only a company that once knew better.

Stay on the line.  
This is the law.  
Keep the hall standing.
