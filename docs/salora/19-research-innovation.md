# SALORA OS — Research & Innovation Constitution

Phase 16.

How SALORA evolves.  
Governance Volume V named that research informs and does not replace judgment.  
This file is the law of studies, pilots, ethics, transfer, and the innovation pipeline.

Not a lab brochure.  
Not a grant template.

Every researcher, PM, AI scientist, and teacher-partner follows this file.

If a study cannot name consent, harm, and what will not be stored, it does not start.

---

# Volume I — Research Philosophy

**Dignity first** — A finding that requires a tape is not a finding we need.

**Learning over novelty** — Week-eight return beats a new guest costume.

**Evidence over theater** — Observation can kill a metric.

**Humility** — A weekend p-value does not ship a child path.

**Transfer** — If it does not change an item, a seat, or a refuse, it was a tour.

**Open where safe** — Methods may be public. Mouths may not.

**No surprise sensors** — Gaze, face, always-on: extra ethics, default off.

**Innovation is a pipeline** — Not a side quest that skips Safety.

# Volume II — Educational Research

Learning System Volume XIII.  
Pilots with teachers. Opt-out. Success is trust and transfer, not a logo.

# Volume III — AI Research

Sandbox. No production memory. No real child roster.  
Red team. Evaluation seats.  
A model that wins a leaderboard and restarts the person loses.

---

# Volume IV — UX Research

Acts, load, return.  
No leading “don’t you love.”  
Notes without raw child speech in shared folders.

---

# Volume V — Product Research

Taxonomy, journey, removable.  
A feature that metrics love and restarts the person is a failed study.

---

# Volume VI — Experimental Design

Pre-register when we claim science.  
Practical significance and harm beside p.  
No A/B on safety, forget, consent, or official child records.  
A/B on copy and layout only when harm is negligible.

---

# Volume VII — Pilot Programs

Contract, DPA, teacher partner, known absences spoken.  
Kill criteria written before start.  
Export and leave tested.

---

# Volume VIII — Ethics

Minors: extra.  
No deception about recording.  
No sale of study speech.  
Consent distinct from product consent. Revocable.  
IRB-equivalent when the study claims general knowledge.

---

# Volume IX — Publications

Truthful. Limits named.  
No child anecdote.  
No “we listen to every word” as a brand.

---

# Volume X — Knowledge Transfer

Findings enter Organizational Memory and, if warranted, Knowledge, Learning, or Engineering standards.  
An unread PDF is not transfer.

---

# Volume XI — Research Infrastructure

Isolated plane.  
Synthetic or consented data.  
Forget honored.  
No vanity join to prod analytics.

---

# Volume XII — Open Science

We may share methods, de-identified aggregates, and open knowledge packages.  
We do not share children.  
Open weights do not excuse a tape.

---

# Volume XIII — Innovation Pipeline

Idea → harm analysis → sandbox → ethics if people → eval → pilot → reviews (Governance IV) → prod.  
Skipping ethics for speed is an incident.  
A hackathon is a sandbox, not a launch.

---

# Volume XIV — Future Research

New methods, same refuse.  
Lifelong studies need lifelong consent.  
A future BCI study is optional, extra, never the price of a lesson.

---

# Volume XV — Research Manifesto

We learn about learning without eating the learner.

Curiosity is a duty. Extraction is not. The best paper we never publish is the one that would have required a child’s mouth in a folder.

What must never change: distinct consent, no A/B on the kernel of trust, observation can veto, transfer into the product, no study speech for sale, innovation that still fails toward the host.

Stay on the line. Ask with care. Change the next item, not the person’s skin.
