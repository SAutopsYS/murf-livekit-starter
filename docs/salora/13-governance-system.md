# SALORA OS — Governance & Operating Constitution

Phase 10.

The Constitution defines the mission.  
The Product defines the platform.  
The Ecosystem defines collaboration.

This file defines how SALORA itself is governed.

Not a company handbook.  
Not an HR manual.  
Not a startup playbook.

The permanent Governance and Operating Constitution.  
Founders, executives, board, product, engineering, education, research, legal, operations, finance, partners, and every future steward follow this file.

Operating Standards named how teams execute on a Tuesday.  
Ecosystem named how partners are refused.  
Platform named how services change.

This file is the law of stewardship: who may decide, what must be reviewed, what must be remembered, and what may never be sold.

If a decision cannot name its owner, its evidence, its review path, and the law it obeys, it is not a decision. It is a mood.

---

# Volume I — Governance Philosophy

SALORA is held, not owned, in the only sense that matters: the hall outlives the people who sit in it.

**Stewardship over ownership**  
A founder, a board, and a buyer may hold equity or office. They do not hold the right to restart a person, keep a tape, or turn the host into a store of souls. Title is temporary. Duty is not.

**Mission before markets**  
The market is a constraint. The mission is the purpose. When a quarter and a child’s dignity conflict, the quarter loses. A company that needs the opposite should not hold this OS.

**Long-term thinking**  
Week-eight return without re-explaining beats day-one delight. A decade of licensed memory beats a week of growth theater. We plan in lives of practice, not in launch weeks alone.

**Educational responsibility**  
We put a mouth in a room with a child. That is a duty before it is a product. Every function exists to keep that duty.

**Trust before growth**  
Growth that costs trust is shrinkage. A leaked mouth, a stranger greeting, a ranked child — these are not “scale problems.” They are governance failures.

**Decentralized expertise**  
The person closest to the evidence holds the first pen: a teacher on a class, a psychometrician on a sample, a privacy engineer on a field. Rank does not invent a fact. Rank may still refuse a harm.

**Evidence-based decisions**  
A slide is not evidence. A loud room is not evidence. A metric that ranks children is not evidence we accept. Observation, studies, validity, and incidents are.

**Responsible innovation**  
New guests, new devices, new partners — after evaluation, after harm analysis, after a way home. Speed that skips Safety, Forget, or fail-closed is not innovation. It is negligence with a demo.

**Institutional memory**  
The organization learns as a person does: it keeps a trail, it forgets what it must not keep (speech), and it does not restart every new leader as a stranger to the law.

**Continuous improvement**  
Defects return like spaced items. Governance reviews itself. A constitution that cannot be amended by a written, hard path will be broken by a quiet one. Amendment is allowed. Drift is not.

# Volume II — Organizational Architecture

Functions are duties, not fiefs.  
A function that cannot name what it must refuse is not a function.

**Executive leadership** — Holds the mission against the quarter. Names owners. Escalates Sev-1. Cannot waive the Constitution for a deal.

**Product** — What exists and why. Owns Product Review. Cannot ship around Educational, Privacy, or Safety review.

**Engineering** — Continuity of the platform. Owns fail-closed, forget-complete, one pipeline. Cannot add a second host “just in case.”

**AI research** — Seats, evaluation, model pins. Cannot put a model in a mouth without Volume XII of the AI System.

**Learning science** — How a mind moves. Can refuse a path that has no evidence. Cannot be overruled by a growth experiment on a child.

**Curriculum** — Bindings of paths and objectives. Cannot mint a course that does not present knowledge objects.

**Knowledge engineering** — The graph, evidence, versions. Cannot publish an uncited live node.

**Design** — The room. Owns Design and Experience review. Cannot trade hierarchy for fashion.

**Accessibility** — Can block a required surface. Their refuse stands.

**Trust & Safety** — Child, harm, abuse, escalation. Sits in front of every mouth. Cannot be a flag in production.

**Security** — Keys, tenancy, supply chain. A demo does not waive them.

**Privacy** — License, minimization, forget, export. A column named transcript is an incident they own with Security.

**Legal** — Contract, DPA, child law, copyright. Demos do not waive residency.

**Operations** — Continuity of the company: planning, incidents, documentation. Protects the thread of work the way the host protects the thread of a lesson.

**Partnerships** — Ecosystem entry. Protects the learner before the logo. Refuse is a success.

**Research** — Studies under ethics. Informs. Does not replace judgment. Does not join vanity to a child’s mouth.

**Community** — Voluntary groups. Exit and dignity. Dissolves rank-and-shame.

**Customer success** — Institutions as colleagues. They do not promise a tape to close a ticket.

**Finance** — Runway and truth in numbers. Cannot design a payment wall that humiliates a child at a host that already knew them. Policy is written.

## Boundaries

A function may advise a neighbor. It may not absorb a neighbor’s refuse.  
Growth is not a function that outranks Trust, Privacy, Learning Science, or Accessibility.  
If it is not in this list, it is a project inside a function, or it is not permanent.

# Volume III — Decision Architecture

Every decision has an owner, evidence, a review path, and a name in the log.  
The Constitution’s hierarchy stands: Human → Learning → Trust → Experience → Technology → Business → Growth → Revenue.

**Strategic decisions** — Board and executive. Mission, markets we will not enter, what we will not sell. Evidence: harm, law, decade-scale duty. Review: board. Cannot invert the hierarchy.

**Product decisions** — Product owner. What to build. Evidence: journey, taxonomy, reviews. Path: Volume IV.

**Educational decisions** — Learning Science + Curriculum. What to teach and how. Evidence: Learning System Volume XIII. Growth cannot own these.

**Technical decisions** — Engineering owner of the layer. RFC. Platform Architecture Volume IX. Continuity of the person is not breakable.

**AI decisions** — AI Research + Trust & Safety. Seat, pin, retire. Evaluation scores. Three live sessions after a host swap. Either can veto.

**Privacy decisions** — Privacy + Legal. New field, new share, new residency. Default is no. A “growth need” is not a lawful basis we invent.

**Accessibility decisions** — Accessibility. Block stands. Appeal is executive plus the function, not a designer alone.

**Research decisions** — Research + Ethics. Protocol, consent, publish. No A/B on safety, forget, or a child’s official record.

**Emergency decisions** — Named incident commander. Speech leak and restart-the-person are Sev-1. Growth is not in the war room before those. Write the decision after the room is safe.

**Deprecation decisions** — Owner of the object. Replacement named. Continuity preserved. Memory and Stop cannot be deprecated.

A decision made in chat and not logged is not institutional. It will be forgotten, then repeated, then denied.

# Volume IV — Product Governance

No feature bypasses constitutional review.  
A “small change” that touches host, memory, voice, child, or forget is not small.

The path, in order. A later stage cannot skip an earlier refuse.

1. **Vision alignment** — Does this make the next attempt cheaper, clearer, or more honest? If no, stop.
2. **Constitutional review** — Laws I–IV and the hierarchy. A single “we’ll fix trust later” fails.
3. **Product review** — Operating Standards Volume VIII. Why it exists, who is harmed, offline, no-voice, removable.
4. **Educational review** — Learning System. Objective, practice, evidence line.
5. **Design review** — Design System Volume XII. One primary, no hover-only, tokens.
6. **AI review** — Seat, prompt layer, fail-closed, evaluation if a model speaks.
7. **Accessibility review** — Required path. Block stands.
8. **Privacy review** — Fields, consent, forget, projections.
9. **Security review** — Authz, secrets, tenancy, threat model if children or export.
10. **Launch approval** — Named humans from the reviews that applied. Scorecard. Ship bar holds. Trust and Learning cannot be zero.

A launch that skipped a required review is rolled back, even if the metric moved.  
The rollback is a governance success.

# Volume V — Research Governance

Research informs product. It does not replace judgment.  
A finding that conflicts with dignity or safety loses, and we write why.

**Educational research** — Learning System and Operating Standards Volume V. Pilots, teachers, observation.

**AI research** — Sandbox, red team, evaluation. No production memory. No real child roster.

**UX research** — Acts and load, not “don’t you love.” Notes without raw child speech in shared folders.

**Platform research** — Reliability, latency, fail-closed. Not engagement theater.

**Longitudinal studies** — Return without re-explaining. Week-eight and year-one. The north star.

**Pilot programs** — Contract, DPA, teacher partner, opt-out. Success is learning and trust, not a logo on a wall.

**Ethics review** — Required for minors, deception, recording, and any new sensor. Distinct consent.

**Publication** — Truthful. No child as an anecdote. No secret sauce that is actually a tape.

**Reproducibility** — Methods and de-identified results where we claim science. A one-weekend p-value is not a ship order.

**Knowledge transfer** — Findings enter Organizational Memory (Volume IX) and, if warranted, the Knowledge or Learning systems. They do not die in a slide.

When metrics love a launch and a single restart-shame story appears, the story can stop the launch. That is research working.

# Volume VI — Operational Architecture

Operations protect continuity — of the product and of the institution.

**Planning** — Written. Laws I–IV first. A quarter of costume is a failed plan.

**Execution** — Small, reversible. Except privacy and safety, which are not experiments.

**Roadmaps** — Layers from the Product Blueprint. Core before Future. A demo that skips Core is not a milestone.

**Incident response** — Speech leak Sev-1. Restart-the-person Sev-1. Named commander. No growth theater in the channel. Postmortem in the Memory (Volume IX).

**Risk management** — Volume XI. Anticipated. Written.

**Quality assurance** — Volume X. Gates, not vibes.

**Documentation** — How to fail, forget, add a guest, export, amend. Not a graveyard of sprints.

**Internal communication** — Writing is the default. If it is not written, it is not a decision. No meeting to protect a restart.

**Knowledge management** — Institutional Memory is the product. Searchable. Access-controlled. No child speech in the wiki.

**Continuous improvement** — Defects spaced. Standards amended on purpose (Volume VII).

Operations that optimize busyness and lose the thread have failed the metaphor of the hall.

# Volume VII — Standards Governance

Standards evolve deliberately, not accidentally.  
Each standard has an owner function and a change path.

| Standard | Source of truth | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Product | Architecture, Blueprint, Product Review | Product |
| Design | Design System | Design |
| Engineering | Platform Architecture, Operating Standards VI | Engineering |
| AI | AI System | AI Research + Trust & Safety |
| Learning | Learning System | Learning Science |
| Assessment | Assessment System | Learning Science + Assessment |
| Accessibility | Design X, Experience VIII, this refuse | Accessibility |
| Security | Platform VII | Security |
| Privacy | Platform VIII, Constitution | Privacy |
| Documentation | This volume + Operations | Operations |

A change to a standard is an RFC: reason, evidence, who is harmed, migration.  
A silent edit of a standard to unblock a launch is an incident.  
“We don’t do that anymore” without an amendment is drift.

The Constitution amends only by Volume XII’s path.  
A standard may not contradict the Constitution. If it does, the standard is void.

# Volume VIII — Ethics & Responsibility

Ethics precede optimization.  
A faster, cheaper, funnier harm is still a harm.

**Educational ethics** — Do not steal the try. Do not rank children. Do not certify sitting. Do not shame Forgotten.

**AI ethics** — Humility, honesty, one host, no invented curriculum, no clinician play, evaluation before a seat.

**Research ethics** — Consent, minors, no deception about recording, no sale of study speech.

**Child protection** — First. Distress stops the drill. Human offer. No isolation dressed as privacy from a parent when the risk is harm.

**Fairness** — Same dignity. Accommodations that keep the construct. No locale as “easier.” No paid tier that buys more humanity — only more guests, never more respect.

**Bias mitigation** — Studies, item review, locale review. A known biased object is retired.

**Transparency** — They know when they are sampled, what is stored, who sees a projection, when a guest enters.

**Sustainability** — Energy and cost are evaluation dimensions. A 10× bill for a 1% costume is refused. We do not burn a forest to look thoughtful.

**Human oversight** — Official claims, certifications, partner entry, model seats, emergencies. A system that cannot name the human is not allowed to act.

**Accountability** — Names in the log. Vacancy of a Sev-1 owner is itself an incident. “The model did it” is not an account.

# Volume IX — Organizational Memory

Organizations learn as people do.  
They keep a trail of what they decided and what they broke.  
They do not keep a lake of children speaking.

**Architectural decisions** — ADRs / RFCs. Layer, data class, dissent, law cited.

**Research findings** — Methods, limits, transfer to product. No raw child speech.

**Product history** — What shipped, what was refused, what was rolled back. The refuse is as valuable as the ship.

**Educational evidence** — Validity, pilots, item-fault. Bound to objects when we can.

**Lessons learned** — After incidents and after quiet failures. Specific. Not a slogan.

**Decision logs** — Owner, date, evidence, review, hierarchy cited. Searchable.

**Deprecation records** — What left, what replaced it, how continuity was kept.

**Incident history** — Class of failure, not the mouth. Speech leak is recorded as a leak, not as a quote.

**Governance reviews** — Periodic: did we skip a path? Did a function lose its refuse? Written.

**Knowledge preservation** — New stewards can find the law and the scars. A leadership change is not a factory reset of memory.

If a new executive cannot find why we refused a tape, we have already begun to fail.

# Volume X — Quality System

Quality is continuously verified.  
A vibe of quality is not a system.

**Product quality** — Continuity, one primary, removable, fail-closed. Reviews passed.

**Educational quality** — Transfer, retention, dignity. Evidence line.

**AI quality** — Evaluation gate. Honesty, Safety, Recovery cannot be 1.

**Design quality** — Tokens, hierarchy, reduced motion, no hover-only.

**Platform quality** — Host SLO, forget-complete, no utterance fields.

**Content quality** — Bound objects, two-human publish, twins.

**Partner quality** — Governance of the Ecosystem. Kill switch used when needed.

**Research quality** — Ethics, reproducibility, no vanity join.

**Operational quality** — Incidents closed, docs alive, decisions logged.

**Governance quality** — Reviews not skipped, amendments written, succession current.

A quality number that requires a rank of children is not a quality number we keep.

# Volume XI — Risk Management

Risk is anticipated, not merely reacted to.  
Each class has an owner and a written “we will not.”

**Technical** — Second pipeline, dual tutors, lost forget, dependency we do not own.

**Educational** — False mastery, stolen proofs, paths without practice, learning-styles myth as policy.

**AI** — Hallucinated scores, costume certainty, guest-of-guest, model swap as personality swap.

**Security** — Keys in the tree, admin as lesson viewer, supply chain.

**Privacy** — Transcript column, partner copy that ignores forget, research join.

**Legal** — Child law, DPA, copyright, residency waived in a demo.

**Operational** — Owner vacancy, undocumented Sev-1, roadmap of costume.

**Reputational** — A child in a sales deck, a rank of schools, a lie about recording.

**Financial** — Payment cruelty, growth that sells a tape, a deal that inverts the hierarchy.

**Global** — A market that requires a bedroom camera, a locale we cannot govern, humanitarian harvest.

A risk we will not take is written.  
A risk we take has a monitor and a kill.  
A risk we discover in production is an incident, then a standard change.

# Volume XII — Organizational Evolution

Growth never compromises purpose.  
Evolution is written.

**New teams** — A new function names its refuse and its neighbor. If it has no refuse, it is a project, not a team.

**New products** — Same host, same license, same identity. A new product that restarts the person has failed the merge of meaning.

**New platforms** — Inherit Design Volume XI and Platform XIII. New sensors optional.

**Acquisitions** — The acquired thing obeys the Constitution or it is not turned on. Their tape, if they had one, is not our feature. Their model does not sit in the host chair without evaluation.

**Partnerships** — Ecosystem law. Logo last.

**International expansion** — Global Governance (Volume XIII). Locale first-class. Residency real.

**Organizational change** — Reorgs do not delete owners of Sev-1. A week without a Privacy owner is an incident.

**Constitutional amendments** — Written. Reason. Harm analysis. Board (or the steward body of the day) plus the functions that hold the refuse being changed. Dissent recorded. A quiet edit of the Constitution is an incident and a void act.

**Leadership transition** — The incoming steward reads the Constitution, this file, the incident history, and the refuse log before they hold a ship button.

**Succession planning** — Named deputies for Trust & Safety, Privacy, Host/voice, and Forget. Personality is not a plan.

The company may be sold. The duty may not be.  
A buyer who needs a tape should be refused, even late.

# Volume XIII — Global Governance

Global scale requires local responsibility.  
A region is not a franchise to weaken the law.

**Regional leadership** — Named humans who can refuse a local deal that costs dignity. They do not invent a second Constitution.

**Local education systems** — Paths and boards as edges. Teachers remain colleagues.

**Regulatory compliance** — Real. Architecture allows region. Sales do not waive it.

**Cultural governance** — Language System and Knowledge locale law. No single-city “global.”

**International partnerships** — Same Ecosystem gate. A ministry is not a reason to add a tape.

**Educational standards** — Mapped, not worshipped. Dignity outranks a framework’s fashion.

**Data sovereignty** — Residency as contracted. Forget completes where the person lives.

**Crisis coordination** — Sev-1 is global. A leak in one region is a leak. Commander named.

**Humanitarian response** — Thinner identity allowed. Harvest of the vulnerable forbidden. Offline first-class.

**Global stewardship** — We do not run a kinder host in a rich city and a watcher in a poor one.

# Volume XIV — Future Organization

The company will change shape. The duty will not.

**AI-assisted governance** — Models may draft RFCs, cluster incidents, and flag drift. They do not approve launches, sit as board, or waive Safety. A future that lets a model amend the Constitution has left the hall.

**Distributed organizations** — Place may scatter. Owners, logs, and refuses may not.

**Global educational cooperatives** — Shared graphs and packages. Open is not ungoverned. Children are not the commons.

**Autonomous operational systems** — Autoscaling and auto-rollback are allowed. Autonomous policy change is not.

**Continuous constitutional review** — Scheduled. Written. Amendment or reaffirmation. Not a ritual poster.

**Planetary learning networks** — If the hall is farther, offline and latency grow. Dignity does not thin.

**Multi-generational stewardship** — A steward who was once a learner here should find the same host duty, not a stranger’s company.

**Open governance models** — We may publish more of the law. We do not publish children. Transparency of rules is not transparency of mouths.

**Future institutions** — New legal forms (steward-ownership, cooperative, public trust) are allowed if they strengthen the duty. A form that makes the tape easier is refused.

**Long-term civilization impact** — We measure ourselves by whether more people can try, miss, and return without shame — not by whether our name is on a sky.

The future may add a legal wrapper we cannot name.  
It may not add a governor who is a model, a market that buys the Constitution, or a generation that never reads the refuse log.

# Volume XV — Governance Manifesto

Institutions exist to protect missions, not themselves.

A company that survives by forgetting why it was built is a shell. A board that protects the board is a club. A founder who cannot be succeeded is a single point of failure wearing a myth. This hall was not made so that an office could persist. It was made so that a person could persist in a practice without being treated as a stranger. When the institution and the mission conflict, the institution changes shape. The mission does not.

Governance preserves trust across generations because trust is not a feeling we relaunch. It is a trail: we did not keep the tape, we did not rank the child, we did not sell the host, we wrote the refuse, we named the owner, we rolled back the clever harm. A child who becomes an adult should be able to open the same hall and find the same duty. If they find a store, we have failed them twice — once as a learner, once as a citizen of the thing we built.

Evidence is stronger than authority. A title does not make a fact. A loud room does not make a study. A model does not make a curriculum. Authority’s only honest use is to refuse a harm when the evidence is not yet in, and to yield when the evidence shows we were wrong. Rank that inverts the hierarchy is not leadership. It is a restart of the law.

Leadership is stewardship. The work is to keep the table quiet, the next try possible, the forget complete, the guest on a way home, and the people who hold the refuses un-bullied. Charisma is optional. The log is not. A steward who needs to be the smartest mouth in the room is in the wrong hall.

Constitutions outlive founders. That is the point. If this file only works when a particular person is in the chair, it is not a constitution. It is a personality. We write so that a stranger, hired in a decade we will not see, can refuse a tape without calling us. We write so that a buyer cannot pretend they were not warned. We write so that the hall can be rebuilt without evicting the people inside it.

What must never change, even when organizations, markets, and technologies evolve:

The mission outranks the market.  
Trust outranks growth.  
A child outranks a contract.  
Every decision has an owner, evidence, and a log.  
No feature bypasses constitutional review.  
Speech leak and restart are Sev-1.  
Ethics precede optimization.  
AI drafts. Humans approve the law and the launch.  
The Constitution amends in the open or not at all.  
Stewards can be replaced. The duty cannot.

The operating system is a hall with a law.

If the law is held, many hands can keep the table.  
If the law is a poster, one quarter can sell the chairs.

Stay on the line.  
Write the decision.  
Refuse the tape.  
Leave the hall standing.
