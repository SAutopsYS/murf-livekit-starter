# SALORA OS — Data Constitution

Phase 14.

Define all platform data.  
Not a warehouse diagram.  
Not a tracking plan for vanity.

The permanent Data Architecture.  
Data, analytics, AI, privacy, and product follow this file.

Knowledge is what can be taught.  
Memory is what was licensed about a person.  
Learning state is where they are on a class.  
Events are what happened without saying what was said.

If a dataset cannot name its class, owner, retention, and whether a mouth could hide in it, it is not a dataset we keep.

---

# Volume I — Data Philosophy

**Purpose first** — A field exists for a try, a license, a contract, or an audit. Not for a future idea.

**Separation** — Knowledge ≠ memory ≠ events ≠ audit. Mixing buckets is a defect.

**Minimization** — Absence over policy.

**Learner ownership** — They can see, export, and erase what is about them. Events about a route are not a biography.

**Truth over volume** — A correct state beats a lake of clicks.

**No ranking purpose** — We do not store data to build a league of children.

**Lineage** — We can say where a number came from. “The model felt it” is not lineage.

**Forget is data work** — Deletion is a first-class pipeline, not a ticket.

---

# Volume II — Data Architecture

Planes: operational, learning, memory, content/knowledge, analytics, audit, configuration, offline cache.

Each plane has a store law (Infrastructure Volume V).  
Analytics may read events, not mouths.  
AI may read licensed projections and knowledge ids, not a hidden diary.

---

# Volume III — Data Models

**Identity** — User, role, tenancy, agent. Host id stable.

**Consent** — Capability, time, receipt, revoke.

**Learning** — Skill/concept state, level, due, last class score, item ids.

**Memory** — Licensed fields only. Listable.

**Session** — Visit id, live flag, item, guest, device takeover. Short-lived.

**Content/knowledge** — Object ids, edges, versions, licenses, `cites`.

**Assessment** — Sample id, class, item ids, outcome, human-path flag. No oral blob.

**Credential** — Claim, issuer, date, graph version, decay rule.

**Event** — Name, time, route, error class, latency. No payload of speech.

A column named `transcript`, `utterance`, `recording`, or `raw_prompt` on a learner path is forbidden.

---

# Volume IV — Event Architecture

Events are facts of the system, not of the mouth.

Allowed: session_start, resume, guest_enter, guest_fail, fail_closed, forget_complete, item_scored (class + result, not the spoken answer), permission_asked, permission_refused.

Forbidden: the words they said, the words we said, OTPs, phones, secrets.

Events are idempotent where writes can double.  
A duplicate attempt event is a defect.

---

# Volume V — Analytics

Improve the next try and the host path.  
Not engagement theater.  
Aggregates for schools and enterprise.  
Empty is drawn.  
No child as a quote.  
Retention and transfer outrank time-on-screen.

---

# Volume VI — Warehousing

A warehouse is a copy with a purpose.  
It does not become a second memory.  
It does not ingest speech “for later NLP.”  
Retention shorter or equal to the source law.  
Forget propagates or the warehouse is disconnected.  
Researchers use the Research plane, not a join of prod vanity.

---

# Volume VII — Data Governance

Every table has an owner, a class, a retention, and a review date.  
New field: Privacy review.  
New share: consent and purpose.  
Catalog is honest. A shadow table is an incident.

---

# Volume VIII — Privacy

Obeys Phase 13.  
In data language: no prohibited columns, receipts on shares, deletion jobs tested, export privacy-safe.

---

# Volume IX — Quality

Accuracy of state. Completeness of required ids. Timeliness of due. Consistency across devices (one live session).  
Item-fault vs learner-fault labeled.  
A pretty dashboard over wrong states is a failed warehouse.

---

# Volume X — Lineage

A score, a due date, a credential, and a retrieved id can be traced to items, versions, and seats.  
Embeddings are lineage of an index, not a source.  
A number without lineage does not face a child as official.

---

# Volume XI — AI Data

Training: no child speech we swore not to keep. No production mouths as fine-tune by default.  
Eval sets: synthetic or consented, privacy-safe.  
Features for personalization: licensed fields only.  
RAG: object ids we have. Empty is empty.  
Logs: route, not prompt body.

---

# Volume XII — Operational Data

Live flags, queues, idempotency keys, health.  
Short retention.  
Not a profile.  
Not a place to park a recording “until the job runs.”

---

# Volume XIII — Future Data

More graphs, more locales, more credentials.  
Not more lakes.  
Personal knowledge spaces remain licensed views.  
A future “foundation dataset” that needs children speaking is refused.

---

# Volume XIV — Data Stewardship

Named stewards per plane.  
Vacancy is an incident.  
Stewards can refuse a field.  
They teach new engineers the forbidden columns the way we teach Stop.

---

# Volume XV — Data Manifesto

Data is a trail of duty, not a harvest.

We will know where a person is on a class, what they licensed, what we issued, and how the route failed — and we will not know the shape of their fear in a waveform we kept for comfort.

What must never change: separate buckets, no utterance columns, forget as a pipeline, lineage for official numbers, warehouse that can die if it cannot honor delete, AI that does not eat the mouth.

Stay on the line. Store the state. Not the soul.
