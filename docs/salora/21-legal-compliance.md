# SALORA OS — Legal & Compliance Constitution

Phase 18.

Legal foundations as duty, not as a sticker.  
Security named privacy architecture.  
Ecosystem named DPAs and partners.  
This file is how law, contract, and regulation bind the hall without becoming an excuse for a tape.

Not a jurisdiction memo.  
Not outside counsel’s letterhead.

Legal, privacy, security, partnerships, finance, and the board follow this file.

If a deal needs a field we refused, the deal is refused.  
If a law required a product we will not be, we do not enter that product shape.

---

# Volume I — Legal Philosophy

**Duty before deal** — A contract cannot waive the Constitution for a child.

**Floor, not ceiling** — Child law, privacy law, and accessibility law are minima. Our refuse may be stricter.

**Honesty in paper** — We do not promise listen-to-every-word. We do not hide recording in a paragraph.

**Portable rights** — Export, delete, leave. Written in the product and the contract.

**No jurisdiction shopping for harm** — A region is not a reason to keep a mouth.

**Counsel advises; stewards decide** — Legal can block a deal. It cannot invent a tape because a customer asked.

**Write the exception** — If we must limit a feature in a country, we write it. We do not silently weaken the host.

---

# Volume II — Global Compliance

Residency capable. DPA before roster.  
Local counsel where we operate.  
Demos do not waive.  
A checklist that permits a lake is the wrong checklist.

---

# Volume III — Educational Regulations

Boards and ministries map to paths and credentials.  
We do not invent academic credit.  
We do not become a covert examiner of a state’s children without their warrant and our human path.

---

# Volume IV — Child Protection Law

Minors: extra review, extra consent where required, extra refuse.  
No sexual content, no grooming, no bedroom proctor camera.  
Mandatory reporting follows local law and our human escalation — we are not a secret.  
Parents get outcomes, not tapes, unless a lawful order we will still minimize.

---

# Volume V — AI Regulations

Disclose that a guest is not a human when the law or dignity requires.  
Pins and evaluation are our evidence of control.  
A regulation that required training on our learners’ mouths by default is a shape we refuse.  
Safety layer stays on.

---

# Volume VI — Privacy Law

Consent, minimization, access, correction, deletion, portability, residency.  
Our architecture aims to make these true, not merely promised.  
Lawful basis is not “growth.”

---

# Volume VII — Accessibility Law

Minima (whatever the local name).  
Our Design and Experience law is stricter: twins, stop, no hover-only.  
A required lesson that fails access is a legal and a product defect.

---

# Volume VIII — Licensing

Packages, software, data, models — a license we can honor.  
We do not teach from a stolen book.  
Partner packages: same.

---

# Volume IX — Copyright

Short citation is not a chapter.  
Legal review when we use another’s work.  
Attribution visible.  
A child’s homework is theirs, not our training set by default.

---

# Volume X — Intellectual Property

We may hold marks and software.  
We do not claim a child’s attempt.  
We do not claim a teacher’s professional judgment.  
Open packages stay open as licensed.

---

# Volume XI — Contracts

SOWs cannot add a transcript field.  
Failed payment policy humane and written.  
Exit and export real.  
A “most favored surveillance” clause is refused.

---

# Volume XII — Governance Compliance

Board and amendment paths (Governance XII, Master Constitution).  
Minutes do not contain child speech.  
Insider access is least privilege.

---

# Volume XIII — Audit

Access, forget, export, partner shares.  
Not mouths.  
External auditors see classes of data, not lessons.

---

# Volume XIV — Future Regulation

Laws will thicken around AI, children, and data.  
We will meet them by absence and by human paths, not by building lakes to “be ready.”  
If the future requires a face to learn, we argue and, if needed, withhold the product rather than comply into harm.

---

# Volume XV — Legal Manifesto

Paper should make the hall safer, not slipperier.

A contract is a promise to a person and a school. It is not a tunnel under the Constitution. Counsel’s job is to keep us in the world without selling the chairs.

What must never change: deals cannot mint a tape, child law is a floor, privacy is architecture, credit is not invented, auditors do not need a mouth, we will leave a market before we become a watcher.

Stay on the line. Sign the duty. Not the loophole.
