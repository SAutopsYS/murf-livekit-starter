# SALORA OS — Quality Constitution

Phase 17.

Organizational excellence as law, not a slogan.  
Governance Volume X named quality kinds.  
This file is how quality is verified, audited, and improved.

Not an ISO sticker.  
Not a scorecard for ranking teams like children.

Every function follows this file.

If a quality claim cannot name a test, a review, or a refuse, it is marketing.

---

# Volume I — Quality Philosophy

**Verify, do not vibe** — Gates exist.

**Dignity is quality** — A beautiful harm is low quality.

**Continuity is quality** — A new hello is a defect.

**Trust and Learning cannot be zero** — Ship bar from the company scorecard.

**Refuse is quality** — A stopped launch can be the highest quality act of the quarter.

**Improve like spacing** — Defects return. We do not only add features.

**No rank of people** — Quality systems do not become leaderboards of teachers or children.

# Volume XV — Quality Manifesto

Excellence is the next try still possible, the forget still complete, the guest still home, the child still unranked.

We will not buy a certificate that lets us skip a refuse. We will not call a festival quality. We will not measure people the way we refused to measure children.

What must never change: gates, Trust and Learning nonzero, refuse as success, no speech in the audit, improvement without leagues.

Stay on the line. Verify the duty.
