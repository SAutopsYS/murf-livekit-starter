Gates (short form — Master Constitution Volume IX is the full list):

- A feature with no place in Architecture and Blueprint is not built.
- A model, agent, or prompt without a seat, layer, and failure path does not enter the room.
- A knowledge object without type, edges, evidence, and version is a file.
- A score or credential without class, items, human path, and validity is decoration.
- A partner without learner benefit, data class, consent, and fail-toward-host does not enter.
- A decision without owner, evidence, review, and law is a mood.
- A column that can hold a mouth is forbidden.
- A folder, service, or dependency without owner, data class, and law does not enter the tree.
- A second voice pipeline does not ship.
- A shared capability used by two products and missing from Core Platform is duplication.
- A learning write without graph object, state transition, and evidence is not implemented.

Do not rewrite the Master to win a quarter.
