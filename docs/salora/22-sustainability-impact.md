# SALORA OS — Sustainability & Impact Constitution

Phase 19.

Long-term societal responsibility.  
Not a CSR brochure.  
Not a carbon sticker on a leak.

The permanent Impact Constitution.  
Leadership, research, product, infrastructure, and partnerships follow this file.

If an impact claim cannot name a person helped and a harm refused, it is advertising.

---

# Volume I — Impact Philosophy

**The try is the impact** — More people able to miss and return without shame.

**Do no harvest** — We do not help a region by eating its children as data.

**Equity is design** — Low-end, offline, script, time. Not a donation after a luxury app.

**Energy is a score** — Evaluation already names it. A 10× bill for costume is refused.

**Wellbeing** — Stop is a right. Streaks do not threaten. We do not trap.

**Honesty** — We do not claim to save a generation from a pilot of a hundred.

**Stewardship** — Impact outlives a campaign. Decade scale.

---

# Volume II — Educational Equity

Same host duty in a rich city and a thin network.  
A locale is not “easier.”  
Fees may buy guests, not humanity.  
Humanitarian programs: thinner identity, full dignity.

---

# Volume III — Accessibility

Impact is whether a required path works for more bodies.  
Twins, type, voice, motor, cognitive load.  
A launch that fails access is a negative impact, however pretty.

---

# Volume IV — Environmental Sustainability

Cost and energy per minute of real learning.  
Do not scale a lake.  
Do not run a thinking theater.  
Prefer smaller seats that recover over vast seats that restart.  
Hardware refresh is not a reason to abandon offline.

---

# Volume V — Digital Wellbeing

Enough is valid.  
No dark continue.  
No notification as a second tutor.  
Exam pressure met with shorter, not louder.  
Silence allowed.

---

# Volume VI — Ethical AI Impact

Humility, no invented curriculum, no clinician play, no rank.  
A more powerful model that steals the try is a negative impact.

---

# Volume VII — Global Education

First-class locales and scripts.  
Boards as edges.  
No single-city default as world.

---

# Volume VIII — Humanitarian Education

Emergency and NGO paths.  
Honest offline.  
No harvest of the vulnerable.  
Short, stoppable, no origin story after a crisis drop.

---

# Volume IX — Community Impact

Voluntary, exitible, unranked.  
Teacher communities without named students.  
We do not extract unpaid child labor as “UGC.”

---

# Volume X — Research Impact

Studies that change an item or a refuse.  
No child as a paper’s ornament.

---

# Volume XI — Economic Sustainability

A company that must sell a tape to live should not live.  
Runway is a duty to the people who trusted us.  
Payment policy does not humiliate a child.

---

# Volume XII — Long-term Stewardship

Succession. Memory of refuses.  
A buyer who needs a tape is refused.  
Impact reports do not include mouths.

---

# Volume XIII — Planetary Education

If the hall is farther, offline and latency grow. Dignity does not thin.  
We do not treat a distant child as a cheaper experiment.

---

# Volume XIV — Future Civilization

We measure ourselves by whether practice without shame is more common.  
Not by whether our name is on a sky.  
A future that automates teaching without a human path for official claims has left this file.

---

# Volume XV — Impact Manifesto

The good we do is a quieter table and a later try that still works.

We will not print a generation on a banner. We will not burn a forest to look thoughtful. We will not call extraction inclusion. We will not sell wellbeing as a streak.

What must never change: equity in the kernel, energy as a score, stop as a right, no harvest in the name of help, no tape in the name of impact, honesty about how small we are and how long the duty is.

Stay on the line. Help the try. Leave the person whole.
